Zerg macro review · ladder 1v1 · EU + NA

Scorpion, 2016–2026

One big jump, then seven flat years. You opened like a masters player and played the middle and late game like a diamond and platinum player in 2020, and you still do in 2026. Your 2026 numbers are your career best, but only by a little over 2020, after about 1,600 more ladder games on EU and NA. More ladder has stopped making you better.

The arc in one table

Averages per era, EU account, weighted by games. Larva figures are per hatchery per minute (E / M / L = early / mid / late game). Reach = share of games that got there.

The real progress happened between 2016 and early 2019: mid-game larva generation rose from 4.3 to 6.1, and 44 drones came almost two minutes earlier. After 2019 the rows barely move. In 2026, mid-game missed larva is 2.40 against 2.74 in 2019–20, and late-game inject uptime is about the same as in 2017–18. You reach 200 supply in 14% of games, against 15% in 2019–20.

2026 by matchup

ZvZ is your most common matchup and has the weakest economy; ZvP and ZvT openings are solid.

ZvZ is 38% of your 2026 EU games. You reach 66 drones in 8% of ZvZ games, and more than 40% never reach 44. Some of that is the matchup. It also means ZvZ results come from early fights and unit control, which larva stats can't measure or fix.

Within the year, March–April was your best macro ever (66 drones 7:51, 150 supply 9:52, 200 supply 11:50). July–August had the most games (144 EU, 60 of them ZvZ) and went backwards on every milestone: more games, worse macro.

What is holding you back

  1. Unspent larva after about 6 minutes

    2.4 missed per hatchery per minute in the mid game, rated diamond, unchanged in substance since 2019.

  2. A late game at platinum level, rarely maxed

    3.0–3.3 missed and about 33% uptime, while only about 40% of games reach the late phase and 14% reach 200 supply. The larva piles up while you are under max. You're either floating minerals or broke because droning stopped. Check unspent resources in a few replays to see which.

  3. An economy that tops out

    66 drones in about 40% of games, 80 drones in about 20%.

  4. ZvZ

    38% of games, the weakest economy, and decided early. Practice the fights.

  5. Swings between periods

    Your best and worst 2026 periods are both in the same year. Playing more games made your macro worse, not better.

Outlook

If you keep practicing the way you have since 2019, expect the same numbers in 2027. You played 1,648 ladder games on EU and NA in 2019–20 and gained almost nothing in the mid game. The gains that are left are all in the 6–12 minute window, where masters players pull away from diamond players. Hit the first two targets and your macro moves from diamond to masters. If you don't, more games won't do it either.
